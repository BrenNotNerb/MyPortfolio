Guiding Ethical Framework

The 5 R's of IT Professionalism

The core standards and ethical duties every aspiring and practicing IT professional must embody.

Pillar 01 🛡️

1. Responsibility

Take ownership of your work and accept accountability for your actions and decisions. Delivering dependable systems requires taking pride in your output and owning up to mistakes when troubleshooting problems.

Pillar 02 🤝

2. Respect

Treat people and their information properly. Communicate politely and professionally. Honor client confidentiality, safeguard private personal data, and maintain supportive, constructive team dialogue.

Pillar 03 ⏱️

3. Reliability

Do what you promise, meet deadlines, and be someone others can count on. Whether writing documentation, delivering sprints, or showing up on time, reliability builds long-term professional trust.

Pillar 04 ⚖️

4. Responsibility for Ethics

Make decisions based on ethical principles, not just what is technically possible. Resist unethical practices such as privacy intrusions, piracy, or deploying unsafe systems for convenience.

Pillar 05 📚

5. Readiness to Learn

Continuously develop your knowledge and skills as technology evolves. Information Technology moves fast; staying receptive to new tools, feedback, and emerging paradigms is essential.
