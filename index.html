Core Principles

The 5 R's of IT Professionalism

The foundational pillars that guide my conduct, mindset, and decisions in technology.

01 / Pillar 🛡️

1. Responsibility

Take ownership of your work and accept accountability for your actions and decisions. Delivering reliable work means standing firmly behind every line of code and every project outcome.

02 / Pillar 🤝

2. Respect

Treat people and their information properly. Communicate politely and professionally. Uphold user privacy, value teammates' perspectives, and foster inclusive collaboration.

03 / Pillar ⏱️

3. Reliability

Do what you promise, meet deadlines, and be someone others can count on. Consistency and dependability form the bedrock of genuine professional trust.

04 / Pillar ⚖️

4. Responsibility for Ethics

Make decisions based on ethical principles, not just what is technically possible. Guard user security, oppose harmful practices, and apply technology for genuine human benefit.

05 / Pillar 📚

5. Readiness to Learn

Continuously develop your knowledge and skills as technology evolves. Embrace change, seek constructive feedback, and remain an eager, humble lifelong learner.
